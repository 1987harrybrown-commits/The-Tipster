Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work correctly and to provide information to site owners.

How We Use Cookies

The Tipster uses only essential and functional cookies. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or sell data to advertisers. We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics platform.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function. They enable core features like authentication and security. You cannot opt out of essential cookies while using the service — without them, you would not be able to log in or access your account.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies remember your preferences to improve your experience. These are not required for the site to work but make it more convenient. They are stored in localStorage rather than traditional cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

Our payment provider Stripe may set cookies when you complete a purchase. These are governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy. We have no control over Stripe's cookies.

We do not use any other third-party cookies. We do not use Facebook Pixel, Google Ads, or any other advertising or tracking cookies.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in. Here's how to manage cookies in common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Changes to This Policy
