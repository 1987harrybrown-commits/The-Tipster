£100 Compounding Bankroll Simulator

How it works: start with £100 and stake the WHOLE bankroll every day, split across that day's bets in proportion to their unit stakes — so 1u is the bankroll divided by the day's total units, not by the number of tips. This is deliberately the most aggressive model there is, and it can reach zero and stay there; on the current ledger it does, in April. It is not what the tips advise, and it is not the basis used for the exposure figures in the deployment notes, which size 1u at 1% of bankroll.
