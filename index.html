Value edge is the gap between our model's estimated true probability of an outcome and the bookmaker's implied probability. A positive edge means the odds on offer are higher than they should be. Consistently backing positive-edge selections is how you build a long-term profitable record.

The engine fetches live odds and strips the bookmaker's margin to find the true implied probability. It then compares that to a statistical model — Poisson-based for football and NHL, pace/scoring model for NBA. When the model probability meaningfully exceeds the market implied probability, the engine publishes that as a value tip.

The live track record shows the running P&L on every settled tip — check the Results section to see the current numbers. Value betting is a long-term approach: individual results vary, but the edge plays out over volume. Following stake recommendations consistently matters more than any single result.
