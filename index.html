Value edge is the gap between what our model thinks the true probability of a result is, and what the bookmaker's odds imply. For example: if our model gives a team a 65% chance of winning, but the bookmaker odds imply only 55%, that is a +10% edge. Consistently backing positive-edge selections is how you build a profitable record over time — not just picking winners.
