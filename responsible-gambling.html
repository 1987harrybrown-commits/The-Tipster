Know the Signs

Gambling can become a problem when it stops being fun. Watch for these warning signs:

Betting more than you can afford to lose
Chasing losses with bigger bets
Gambling to escape stress or problems
Lying to others about your gambling
Borrowing money to gamble
Neglecting work, family or responsibilities
Feeling anxious or irritable when not gambling
Unable to stop even when you want to

Quick Self-Assessment

Answer honestly. If you answer yes to 2 or more, consider speaking to a professional.

Have you ever felt guilty about the way you gamble?
Have you ever needed to gamble with more money to get the same feeling?
Has gambling ever caused financial problems for you or your household?
Have you ever tried to stop or reduce your gambling without success?
Have you ever lied to someone close to you about your gambling?

Based on the PGSI (Problem Gambling Severity Index) questionnaire.

Tools to Stay in Control

All UK-licensed bookmakers are required to offer responsible gambling tools. Use them:

  • Deposit limits — set a maximum daily, weekly or monthly deposit
  • Loss limits — cap how much you can lose in a period
  • Time limits — limit how long you spend gambling per session
  • Reality checks — pop-up reminders of time spent
  • Self-exclusion — block yourself from gambling sites for a set period
  • GamStop — self-exclude from all UK licensed operators at once at gamstop.co.uk
