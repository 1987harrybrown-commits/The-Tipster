Free Football Betting Tips Today

Our football tips cover six competitions: the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1 and the Champions League. The Premier League is the most liquid football betting market in the world, which means bookmakers generally price it efficiently. We find our edge in correctly modelling attack/defence strength mismatches, home advantage and head-to-head records that bookmakers underweight.

Champions League tips are published on match days. The model treats a European tie the same way it treats a domestic one — attack and defence strength from that competition's own table, form, rest, absences and head-to-head — with no separate knockout or continental adjustment. There is no Europa League coverage.

Free NHL & NBA Tips — Why These Markets?

Ice hockey and basketball are our primary markets because bookmakers are less accurate in their pricing of these sports compared to Premier League football. Lower public interest means less efficient markets — and more opportunity for a data-driven model to find genuine edge.

NHL in particular offers daily betting opportunities throughout a long season, with consistent model performance across the full schedule. NBA coverage runs from October through the Finals in June.

Free Betting Tips FAQ

Are the free tips the same quality as Pro tips?
Yes — free tips are simply the top 3 by confidence from the same model that generates all tips. Pro members see more tips, not better ones.

How often are free tips updated?
The card is rebuilt every 15 minutes, and odds are refreshed twice a day at 06:00 and 13:00 UK. The value edge recalculates and tips may enter or exit the free selection.

Do I need an account for free tips?
No account required to view the top 3 free tips on the homepage. You need a free account to receive daily email delivery.

What bookmakers do you use?
We take prices from an aggregated odds feed covering UK-licensed bookmakers, and publish the best available price we can see for each tip. Where the feed identifies the book offering it, that book is named on the tip.
